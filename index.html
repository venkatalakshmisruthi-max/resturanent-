Our Story

Ember & Oak began in 2012 as a ten-table kitchen built around one idea: cook over real fire and let good ingredients speak for themselves.

Our vegetables come from farms within 100 km, our meat is sourced from small producers, and our bread is baked every morning.

Chef Rohan Mehta leads a team trained in classical technique, with fifteen years of experience in kitchens across India and Europe.

  • Fresh, local, seasonal ingredients
  • Wood-fired cooking and house-made breads
  • Warm service in a relaxed, candle-lit room
